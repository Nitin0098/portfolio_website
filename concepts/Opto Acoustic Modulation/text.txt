TECHNICAL CHALLENGES
Now, creating a true 3D hologram technology that could create 3d images in air without requiring reflectors or panel we need to create 'pixels' in air 
That is increadibly difficult because it requires us to produce light from a localised point in air out of nothing or create a microscopic object in air that could reflect light, both of these options are currently impossible or I thought it was. When a light passes through one medium to another with different density, light bends this property is called refraction of light, we can use this property and create a density gradient for a microscopic volume of air in air, turns out we already do this, the answer is sound waves , sound waves are basically regions of high and low density in air. 

This project uses directional interference of ultrasonic sound waves to create localised regions of high or low density in air,  and then a laser is fired to that region with distinct density, and the laser bends due to refraction of light and can reach our eyes, that's the basic idea. Few months later I got to know something like this already exists for years and it's called as 'Opto-Acoustic Modulation'. It's great to realise , I reached deep tech concepts that already exists independently Without any exposure to prior technologies, 

This technology is still very immature, faces some significant challenges:
1)  the density gradient produced by current directional speakers are tiny.
(Calculation of how much density we need and what current speakers can produce)
2)  algorithm to synchronize the operations of this technology between creating pixels and hownmany pixels their shape in 3d space , arrangement of pixels, and when to fire the laser , etc.
3)  computational power needed to run all of this.
