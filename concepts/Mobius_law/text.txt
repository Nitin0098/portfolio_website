Mobius law,

I call this mobius law, it's a phenomenon I discovered while experimenting with mobius strips few years ago, 

If we take a strip of paper and rotate one end (by 180 degrees) of the paper keeping the other end fixed and glue the ends together we get a mobius strip
In this case The phase difference between same plane  on both ends is π

But if the phase difference between same plane on both ends is nπ

And then, we cut the strip by the length of the strip of paper, 

We observe an interesting pattern 

If n is even : we get 2 rings(strips with ends glued together) with nπ phase difference between the end planes

If n is odd we get 1 strip of 2(n+1)π phase difference 

Even the strip doesn't wants it's offspring to be odd. 

Note :  maybe not a new discovery but a mathematical topological result that I conceived, anyways a good personal discovery for me.
