Concept 01: Cognitive Architecture

Cognitive Architecture posits that human-computer interaction should mirror internal cognitive flow: reducing sensory distraction and highlighting pure conceptual relationships.

Inspiration

Inspired by neural network topologies and natural constellation dynamics, Concept 01 removes the artificial fragmentation of menus in favor of unified relational networks.

Observing how the human mind associates nodes through spatial intuition led directly to the concentric focus model demonstrated here.

Conceptual Depth & Implications

In practice, this mental model replaces deep hierarchical silos with fluid contextual layers. Information is surfaced only when intentional focus is applied, maintaining an uncluttered cognitive workspace.
